## What NOT to do

- Don't pay for "submit to 1000 directories" services. Most of those are link farms that hurt rather than help.
- Don't reuse the exact same description verbatim everywhere — Google flags duplicate content. Rotate the short / medium / long variants.
- Don't put a "submit my site" URL in our own footer pointing back to these directories — it's circular and looks SEO-spammy.
