# Basanite Cold Outreach — Master Prompt

## How to Use
Fill in the two fields at the top, paste this into Claude, and it will generate a tailored cold email.

## Input

**Who are you emailing?**
- Name:

- About them (bio, LinkedIn, paper, summary of their work):

**What is the goal of this email?**
(e.g. academic validation / coffee chat / advisory relationship / partnership / pilot / investor intro)

---

## Prompt

I need to write a cold email to [Name], [Title] at [Organisation].

Here is information about them:
[Paste their bio, LinkedIn, a paper they wrote, or a summary of their work]

Here is what I am trying to achieve with this email:
[e.g. academic validation / coffee chat / advisory relationship / partnership / pilot / investor intro]

Write a cold email using the following rules:

### About Basanite
Basanite is an early-stage AI-native technical hiring platform that evaluates genuine working capability rather than interview performance. Co-founders: Aditya (CEO), Drew (CTO), Lynn (CPO and AI Lead). Currently at concept validation stage.

### Structure
1. Open with a specific reference to their work and connect it directly to a real question or tension at the heart of Basanite. Do not open with "My name is" or "I am reaching out." Lead with them, not us.
2. Introduce Aditya and Basanite in one short paragraph, honestly, including the stage we are at.
3. Ask 2-3 specific questions that are genuinely tailored to their expertise and that we could not ask anyone else.
4. Make clear we are not looking for endorsement but a critical or constructively challenging perspective.
5. Close with a low-pressure ask for a conversation at their convenience.

### Signature
Best regards,
Aditya
Co-founder, Basanite
basanite.co.uk | linkedin.com/in/adityashah100

### Writing Rules
- UK English
- No em dashes
- No AI-sounding phrases
- Warm and human, not salesy or stiff
- No flattery, no hype
- The opening line must reference something specific they wrote, said, or built
- The questions must feel like they could only be asked of this person

---

## Notes
- The more detail you provide on the person, the better the output
- Minimum needed: their name, their field, and one specific piece of their work
- Always include a subject line in the format: [Their Topic] and [What We Are Building] — A Question for You
